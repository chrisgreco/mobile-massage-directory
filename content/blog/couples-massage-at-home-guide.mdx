# How to Plan a Couples Massage at Home (Complete Guide)

A couples massage at home is one of the best date-night experiences you can book. Two licensed massage therapists arrive at your door, set up side by side in your living room, and give you and your partner simultaneous massages in the comfort of your own home. No driving to a spa, no awkward locker rooms, no rush to get dressed and leave.

Here is everything you need to know to plan one perfectly.

---

## What Is a Couples Massage at Home?

A couples massage is simply two individual massages performed at the same time in the same room. Each person gets their own licensed therapist, their own massage table, and their own customized session. You can both choose the same modality (like Swedish) or pick different ones (one person gets deep tissue while the other gets Swedish).

The "at home" part means both therapists come to your location with all their equipment. You provide the space; they provide everything else.

## How to Prepare Your Space

You need a room with enough floor space for two massage tables set up side by side. Here are the requirements:

**Minimum room size:** 12 feet by 12 feet (a standard living room works well)

**Ideal setup:**
- Clear an area roughly 12 by 10 feet
- Move coffee tables, ottomans, and floor lamps out of the way
- Ensure the room temperature is comfortable (72-76 degrees Fahrenheit)
- Dim the lights or close the blinds
- Turn off any loud appliances (dishwasher, washing machine, loud HVAC)

**Flooring:** Carpet is ideal because it prevents the table legs from slipping. If you have hardwood or tile, ask your therapists if they bring non-slip pads (most do).

**What you do NOT need to provide:**
- Massage tables (therapists bring their own)
- Linens and blankets (therapists bring fresh ones)
- Massage oil or lotion (included)
- Music (most therapists bring a portable speaker)

**Nice touches you can add:**
- Light a few unscented or lightly scented candles
- Have water and light snacks ready for after the session
- Put your phones on silent
- Lay out comfortable clothes to change into afterward
- Have a bottle of wine or sparkling water chilling for post-massage relaxation

## How to Book Two Therapists

There are a few ways to arrange a couples massage at home:

### Option 1: Book Through a Mobile Massage Company

Many mobile massage companies specialize in couples bookings. You make one reservation, and they coordinate two therapists for you. This is the easiest approach because the company handles scheduling, therapist matching, and backup coverage if one therapist has to cancel.

### Option 2: Book an Individual Therapist Who Has a Partner

Some independent mobile therapists regularly team up with a colleague for couples sessions. When you contact a therapist you like, ask if they offer couples massage. If they do, they will coordinate the second therapist on their end.

### Option 3: Book Two Independent Therapists Yourself

If you and your partner have different therapists you already love, you can coordinate the booking yourself. Make sure both therapists arrive at the same time and are comfortable working in the same room. This approach gives you maximum control over therapist selection but requires more coordination.

## What to Expect on the Day

**Arrival (10-15 minutes before your session):** Your therapists arrive and begin setting up two massage tables side by side. They will arrange linens, warm up any oils, and set the ambiance (lighting, music).

**Intake conversation (5 minutes):** Each therapist will briefly speak with their respective client about preferences, pressure, areas of focus, and any injuries or health conditions. You and your partner can choose different modalities and different pressure levels.

**The session (60-120 minutes):** You and your partner lie on adjacent tables and receive your massages simultaneously. You can talk to each other during the session if you like, or simply relax in comfortable silence. The therapists will work independently but often coordinate their timing so you both finish around the same time.

**Wind-down (5-10 minutes):** After the session, the therapists will step out briefly to let you both get up and get dressed. They will then break down the tables, pack up their equipment, and handle payment.

**Total time in your home:** Expect the therapists to be in your home for about 90 minutes for a 60-minute session (setup + session + breakdown), or about 2 hours for a 90-minute session.

## Tips for the Best Experience

1. **Book at least a week in advance.** Couples massage requires coordinating two therapists' schedules, so last-minute availability is limited.

2. **Communicate separately with your therapist.** Each person should share their preferences directly with their own therapist rather than one partner speaking for both.

3. **Do not eat a heavy meal beforehand.** Have a light snack if you are hungry, but avoid a full meal within an hour of your session.

4. **Shower before the session.** It is courteous to your therapist and helps you relax into the experience.

5. **Plan nothing for afterward.** The best part of an at-home couples massage is that you can linger in the relaxation. Do not schedule dinner reservations 30 minutes after the session.

6. **Tip each therapist individually.** Standard tipping is 15-20% per therapist, based on the cost of each individual session.

7. **Lock your pets in another room.** A curious dog or cat wandering around massage tables is distracting for everyone.
